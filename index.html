About

I'm a Robotics Engineering graduate from Vietnam with a strong passion for robotics and solving challenging engineering problems. My interests lie primarily in locomotion, loco-manipulation, and multi-robot collaboration.

My current research and development focus on sim-to-real transfer for humanoid locomotion, terrain-aware navigation, and whole-body control. I also work on developing real-time C++ deployment pipelines for dexterous manipulation on physical robots, bridging the gap between simulation and real-world applications.
